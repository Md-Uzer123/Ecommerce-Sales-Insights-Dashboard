# Retail Sales Analytics - Vrinda Store (2022)

## Objective
Vrinda Store wants to create an annual sales report for 2022 to understand their customers and grow more sales in 2023.

## Key Business Questions Answered
- Compare sales and orders using a single chart.
- Which month got the highest sales and orders?
- Who purchased more: Men or Women?
- Top 10 states contributing to sales.
- Relation between age and gender based on orders.
- Which channel is contributing to maximum sales?

## Key Insights
- **Gender:** Women are more likely to buy compared to men (~65%).
- **Geography:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 states (~35%).
- **Demographics:** The adult age group (30-49 yrs) is the max contributing (~50%).
- **Channels:** Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra are the max contributing channels (~80%).

## Final Conclusion & Recommendation
To improve Vrinda Store sales in 2023, the strategy should be:
> **Target women customers of the age group (30-49 yrs) living in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh by showing ads/offers/coupons available on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.**
